# Masters courses
## M3C3 Machine Learning Infrastructure Management 5 ECTs 

Time and location 

For specific dates and locations of the course, please refer to the Lukkarit calendar. 

 

Content scheduling 

The course's Moodle environment includes a comprehensive course progression plan that outlines the scheduling of the content and the planned sequence of topics and activities.  

The course will cover the broad areas such as Infrastructure Setup and Automation, Model Deployment and Orchestration, and Performance Optimization and Cost Management. The theory is discussed in connection with each of these areas as they are introduced. 

 

Learning Outcomes 

This course is part of the module Productization of Machine Learning Models. 
 
In this course you will acquire the skills to work with cloud solutions by applying Infrastructure-as-code (IaC) principles to define, manage, and provision infrastructure for machine learning pipelines. You will gain hands-on experience with virtualization and containerization technologies used in model deployment and serving. The course will enable you to understand and utilize tools for workflow orchestration within machine learning pipelines. You will also learn how to optimize costs, throughput, and latency of model inference in serving systems. 

 

Content 

After completing this course, you will be able to: 
- work with a given cloud solution, applying infrastructure-as-code (IaC) to define, manage and provision infrastructure for machine learning pipelines 
- work with given virtualization and containerization technologies used during model deployment and serving 
- understand and use tooling for workflow orchestration in machine learning pipelines 
- optimize costs, throughput and latency of model inference in serving systems 

Teaching methods 

The teaching methods include in-class lectures, practical tasks preceded by relevant theory. In-class lectures are utilized to deliver essential concepts and encourage interactive learning. Practical tasks provide hands-on experience to reinforce theory and develop practical skills. Practical tasks are implemented as teamwork.

The allocated independent workload in your timetable should be effectively utilized to deepen your understanding of the subject matter and complete assignments. This independent work time provides an opportunity for self-directed learning, allowing you to explore the course material in greater depth and engage in additional study

All learning materials and information about relevant literature such as books are available in Moodle (learn.hamk.fi)

Assessment criteria 

This course is assessed on a scale of 0 – 5 based on individual tasks and teamwork. Comprehensive grading guidelines are available in the Moodle platform, providing detailed information on the assessment criteria and expectations.

This course is part of the module Foundations of Machine Learning

Also, you will learn to acquire hands-on experience with big data technologies to process and analyze large datasets, applying theoretical concepts to real-world scenarios and ensuring data accuracy and security. 

 

Content

- utilize cloud computing in data engineering tasks

- choose appropriate Data Storage and Management solution based on specific use cases: including, NoSQL databases, and data lakes 
- implement strategies for data quality and governance to ensure data accuracy, consistency, and security. 
- gain hands-on experience with big data technologies to process and analyze large datasets.

## M4C3: Applied Natural Language Processing, 5 ECTS 

Learning goals: 

In this course you will learn about text preprocessing to prepare raw text data for analysis. You will understand and use word embeddings to represent text as vectors. You will implement, train and debug deep learning models for various language understanding tasks. You will develop and extend neural network models for applications like machine translation and dialogue agents. You will apply modern language model architectures to enhance language understanding and processing capabilities.  

Learning Outcomes: 

After completing this course, you will be able to: 

Perform text preprocessing to prepare raw text data for analysis. 

Understand and utilize word embeddings to represent text data in vector form. 

Implement, train, and debug deep learning models for a range of language understanding tasks. 

Develop and extend neural network models for machine translation, dialogue agents, and other NLP applications. 

Apply modern language model architectures to enhance language understanding and processing capabilities.

Explore and experiment with simple image classification tasks using neural networks, gaining hands-on experience with basic image recognition models.
Explore Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), understanding their architecture and learning how they are applied to more complex image classification problems.
Learn the fundamentals of training CNNs, including the use of datasets such as Rock, Paper, Scissors, and evaluate model performance.
Understand and apply Transfer Learning techniques, leveraging pretrained models for specific tasks to enhance the performance of your neural networks.
Work on a small project, applying the concepts learned throughout the course to solve a specific problem with a provided dataset.
Prepare for the final questionnaire by consolidating your knowledge and ensuring a strong grasp of the course material.
